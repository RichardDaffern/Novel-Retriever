- Novel Updates records **412 web-novel chapters + 69 side stories** for the completed main story, two after-story chapters on hiatus, and 16 Japanese light-novel volumes. It marks the series licensed and not completely translated.

Sources: [Novel Updates](https://www.novelupdates.com/series/the-death-mage-who-doesnt-want-a-fourth-time/) · [FreeWebNovel](https://freewebnovel.com/novel/the-death-mage-who-doesnt-want-a-fourth-time) · [One Peace Books](https://www.onepeacebooks.com/jt/Yondome.html).
