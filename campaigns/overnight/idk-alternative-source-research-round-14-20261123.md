- [WuxiaWorldEU](https://wuxiaworld.eu/novel/rebound-a-2nd-chance) and [ReadNovelEU](https://www.readnovel.eu/novel/rebound-a-2nd-chance) show 323 entries numbered 1–324 and explicitly note that chapter 69 is missing.

The mirror is incomplete; the author's WebNovel publication does not grant this project archive permission. Hold.
