## Next steps and safeguards

1. Verify an exact-title **NovelFull book listing first**; only fall back to FreeWebNovel or another supported site if NovelFull has no verified matching listing. A matching slug alone is never proof of an existing listing.
2. Verify the **author or original work identity** and edition where possible; avoid conflating translated Chinese works, fanfiction and similarly named works.
3. Resolve screenshot-truncated titles using a credible complete title plus author reference before any automatic download.
4. Where a source is found, examine chapter numbering, access limitations, completeness and archiving permission. Do not bypass login, anti-bot controls, paywalls or access restrictions.
5. Do not alter, restart or rerun the already-completed one-shot 10-title fanfiction dispatcher. Schedule any newly verified candidates separately with de-duplication and small batches.
6. Preserve failed downloads as incomplete until chapter integrity and complete registry publication are both verified.

The evidence URLs above are source references, not claims that automated extraction is supported or permitted.
